====== Hires FLI ======

Same as [[base:fli|FLI]] but in hires mode (set by $d016). Now the FLI-bug area always becomes visible, since all pixel codes there are represented by $ff -> bright gray. The bug has to be covered with sprites, or merged to other parts of the screen by using bright gray as background, borders etc.

====== Books on C64 programming ======

This section contains various books related to C64 programming.
Similar to the [[magazines:start|Magazines section]] this section of the wiki consists of material which has once been released as wholes, not intended to be modified. This should be respected, and corrections/comments and so on should rather be put ouside the texts than inside.

It should be noted that a huge bunch of useful books are available at [[http://project64.c64.org|Project64]] and [[http://www.bombjack.org/commodore/|DLH's site]]. Check them!


====== PDF books ======

All these books are OCR'ed, so you can actually search for text snippets in them, even though they are shown as scanned images in your PDF reader.

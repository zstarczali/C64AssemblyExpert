====== StarTrek shooter game (beta). ======

Shoot the Borg cubes with photon torpedoes.
This is the unlimited lives version.
Check out the starry background with three different speed of stars.
After you reach 100 points, two enemies will come.
My top score with one life is 25000.

Developed with TMPx cross assembler.

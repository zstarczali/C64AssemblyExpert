====== C64Amp - Music player ======

New version is released with 11 musics!

The musics were disassembled with my advanced dcc6502 program.
Then they were relocated in the memory.
Then crunched with ByteBoozer.
So these 11 musics are only 100 blocks.

Control it with joystick port 1.

Developed in TMPx cross assembler

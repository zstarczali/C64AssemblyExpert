====== 40 Bytes FLI Displayer ======

This is a simple, supersmall (A)FLI Displayer - written by Crossbow

Bitmap at $4000, Videorams at $6000-

<code>
.C:1000   8D 11 D0   STA $D011
.C:1003   AD 11 D0   LDA $D011
.C:1006   4D 12 D0   EOR $D012
.C:1009   C9 6A      CMP #$6A
.C:100b   D0 F6      BNE $1003
.C:100d   20 A5 EE   JSR $EEA5
.C:1010   8E 20 D0   STX $D020
.C:1013   A0 38      LDY #$38
.C:1015   A9 80      LDA #$80
.C:1017   78         SEI
.C:1018   8D 18 D0   STA $D018
.C:101b   8C 11 D0   STY $D011
.C:101e   E8         INX
.C:101f   F0 DF      BEQ $1000
.C:1021   69 10      ADC #$10
.C:1023   10 EE      BPL $1013
.C:1025   C8         INY
.C:1026   D0 EF      BNE $1017
</code>
